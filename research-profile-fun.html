BACKGROUND

Research grounded
in people.

I am an independent researcher working at the intersection of medical sociology, family dynamics, and gender studies. My work explores how social structures — family, gender norms, and institutional healthcare — shape health outcomes and everyday life.

I use qualitative and mixed-methods approaches to understand how gendered expectations within families translate into disparities in access, diagnosis, and care.
