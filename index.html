Background

Crossing borders, disciplines,
and bodies of knowledge.

My research interests centre on gender and women's health, with a growing interest in cancer care in primary care. I have hands-on experience in both quantitative and qualitative research, and am currently working as a Research Assistant at SPriNT (Singapore Primary Care Cancer Network), Family Medicine, NUS.

Beyond research, I have worked across startup and corporate environments in retail, fashion, and advertising, as well as with NGOs, social workers, children, and minority communities.

Having lived and studied in China, Australia, the UK, and now Singapore, I bring a global, culturally sensitive perspective to interdisciplinary health research.

Research themes

Three intersecting
questions.

🩸
01

Bodies, Stigma & Cultural Norms

How do cultural narratives shape the way women understand and experience their own bodies — from menstruation to postnatal recovery — and what does this mean for health-seeking behaviour?

→
👩‍👧
02

Gender, Family & Reproductive Health

How do gendered expectations within Chinese families — around confinement, caregiving, and motherhood — intersect with institutional medicine to shape women's postnatal wellbeing?

→
🎗️
03

Cancer Care in Primary Care

How can primary care settings better support cancer patients and their families — and what role do gender, culture, and social context play in shaping the experience of diagnosis and recovery?
